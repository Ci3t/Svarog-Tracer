# 🌊 Kiyo Mode — Masterclass Guide

> **Who is this for?** Anyone using Kiyo Mode and wanting to understand every term, signal, and strategy decision clearly.

---

## 1. What Is Kiyo Mode?

The game groups 4xxx rolls into **pairs** each session. Two specific rolls appear far more often together, while the other two fill the gaps.

**Your goal:** Identify which pair is dominant → bet on it → repeat.

---

## 2. The 3 Pairings

Every session belongs to exactly **one** of these:

| Column   | Name         | 🟢 Green Side  | 🟡 Amber Side  |
|----------|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| **41/44** | Outer/Inner  | 41 & 44        | 42 & 43        |
| **42/44** | Odd/Even     | 42 & 44        | 41 & 43        |
| **43/44** | Low/High     | 43 & 44        | 41 & 42        |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **44 appears in the green side of ALL three pairings.** You cannot tell which pairing is active from 44 alone. Wait for 41, 42, or 43 to appear.

---

## 3. Reading the Color Table

The table shows the last 12 rolls with 3 columns (one per pairing).

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 🟢 **Green** | Roll belongs to top-pair side |
| 🟡 **Amber** | Roll belongs to bottom-pair side |

**What to look for:**
1. Find the column with the **longest unbroken green runs**
2. The **★ gold star** marks the auto-detected active column
3. Streaks of **×4 or more** = strong signal → bet that side

---

## 4. The Noise Rule

> [!TIP]
> After every ~N green rows, expect **1-2 amber noise rolls** before green returns. **This is normal.** Do NOT switch columns.

```
🟢🟢🟢  →  🟡🟡  →  🟢🟢🟢  →  🟡  →  🟢🟢🟢
 3 greens     noise      3 greens  noise    3 greens
```

The "noise" fills the gap between runs. If you know N=3, you can even predict the noise is coming after 3 consecutive greens.

## 5. Wave Verdicts — Every Term Explained

### 🏆 DOM (Dominant)
- **Meaning:** One side appears ≥60% of ALL session rolls. Pairing is locked.
- **What to do:** Bet that side every single roll.
- **Example:** `DOM Even (67%)` → Bet 42 or 44 every roll.

---

### 🤚 HOLD (Hold current side)
- **Meaning:** Current side's run is still going (e.g. run 2/3). The game has not flipped yet.
- **What to do:** Keep betting the same side you've been on.
- **Example:** `HOLD High (run 2/3)` → 43 & 44 came twice in a row. Expect one more. Keep betting 43/44.

---

### 🔄 FLIP (Switch sides)
- **Meaning:** The run hit N (e.g. 3/3 complete). The next roll belongs to the OTHER side.
- **What to do:** Switch your bet to the opposite side immediately.
- **Example:** `FLIP → Low (run 3/3)` → 43/44 hit 3 times in a row. Now bet 41 or 42.

> [!WARNING]
> Switch AFTER you see FLIP, not before. Betting the opposite side early (on run 2/3) is the most common mistake.

---

### ⚡ LIKELY (Probably but unsure)
- **Meaning:** The pairing is not confirmed yet — early session or two columns look too similar.
- **What to do:** Bet smaller (half). Wait 2-3 more rolls for confirmation.
- **Example:** `⚡ LIKELY Even` → Probably 42/44 but not locked. Use low-value items.

---

### ⏳ WAIT (Not enough data)
- **Meaning:** Less than ~4 rolls in. Not enough to find any pattern.
- **What to do:** Skip this roll. Come back in 2-3 more rolls.

---

### ⚠️ SKIP (Chaotic session)
- **Meaning:** No consistent pattern detected. Rolls jumping randomly between all sides.
- **What to do:** Don't bet at all. Wait for the session to settle.

---

## 6. Session Mode Badges

The small badge in the 🌊 Wave card shows the **detected pattern type**:

| Badge | Mode | What It Means | How to Play |
|-------|------|---------------|-------------|
| 🏆 DOM | Dominant | One side ≥60% of all rolls | Bet that side every roll |
| 🔄 RUN·N2 | Run N=2 | Rolls swap every 2 | After 2 same-color, expect flip |
| 🔄 RUN·N3 | Run N=3 | Rolls swap every 3 | After 3 same-color, expect flip |
| 🔄 RUN·N4 | Run N=4 | Rolls swap every 4 | After 4 same-color, expect flip |
| 〰️ ALT | Alternating | Rolls alternate every roll | Bet opposite of last roll |
| ⚡ AMBIG | Ambiguous | 2 columns too close to call | Bet smaller, wait 2-3 rolls |
| ⚠️ CHAOS | Chaotic | No pattern at all | Don't bet |
| ⏳ BUILD | Building | Not enough rolls yet | Wait |

---

## 7. Two Signals You'll See

### 📊 TABLE % (Short-term)
- Counts how many of the **last 10-15 rolls** fell on one side
- Best used when you have a **×4+ streak** (strong visual signal)
- A high dom% from only 3-4 rolls = weak signal on its own

### 🌊 WAVE (Whole session)
- Analyses **every roll since you started** the session
- Needs ~8+ rolls to fully confirm pairing
- The **★** appears when confidence ≥ 60%
- More reliable for longer sessions (10+ rolls)

---

## 8. Trust Hierarchy

```
Streak ×5+  AND  Wave agree    →  🔥 Maximum confidence  →  Full bet
Table streak ×4                →  ✅ High confidence      →  Full bet
Wave 🏆 DOM (8+ rolls)         →  ✅ High confidence      →  Full bet
HOLD + no streak               →  🟡 Medium              →  Normal bet
Wave ⚡ LIKELY                 →  ⚠️ Low confidence       →  Half bet
WAIT or SKIP                   →  ❌ No signal            →  Skip
```

> [!NOTE]
> **The golden rule:** A **×5+ visual streak** beats everything — even if Wave disagrees. A run of 5 same-color rows is visual proof. Trust it.

---

## 9. Quick Decision (3 Steps)

```
STEP 1 ─── Is there a ×4 or more streak in the ★ column?
            YES → Follow that color immediately.
            NO  → Go to Step 2.

STEP 2 ─── Does Wave say DOM or HOLD?
            YES → Follow the FOLLOW → signal.
             NO → Is it LIKELY? Bet half.
                  Is it WAIT/SKIP? Skip this roll.

STEP 3 ─── Do TABLE and Wave agree on the same side?
            YES → Full confidence. Bet well.
             NO → Bet smaller, or wait 1-2 more rolls.
```

---

## 10. Common Mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | Why it's wrong | ✅ What to do instead |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Switching columns on amber | Amber = noise, not pattern change | Stay on ★ column |
| Trusting 75% dom% from 4 rolls | 3/4 = tiny sample | Wait for ×4+ streak |
| Betting full on ⚡ LIKELY | Pairing not locked | Use half bet |
| Betting during WAIT/SKIP | No pattern = 50/50 | Skip entirely |
| Switching before FLIP shows | You're early — run unfinished | Switch AFTER the FLIP signal |

---

## 11. Short Summary

> Find the **★ column** → Look for long green streaks or Wave **DOM** → Bet that side → Wait for noise → Bet again.

The longer the session runs, the more accurate both the Wave and TABLE signals become. For sessions under 10 rolls, favour the TABLE streak. For sessions with 10+ rolls, the Wave becomes the primary signal.
